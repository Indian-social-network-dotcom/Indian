Indian Life World mein wapas jaayein

Automatic 3D City Builder

Yahan map par kuchh draw nahi karna hai. Jagah ke coordinates aur area ka size pehle se diya gaya hai. Generate dabane par available OpenStreetMap roads aur building footprints se 3D city model banega.

Pehla test: Kapashera Border, New Delhi. Area 400 × 400 metre. Zaroorat par baad mein coordinates aur size badal sakte hain.

2. Is model mein kya aayega?

  • OpenStreetMap mein darj asli sadkein aur galiyan
  • OpenStreetMap building footprints se 3D buildings
  • Jahan available ho, building height, floors aur colour tags
  • Ek GLB file, jise Indian Life World ke Three.js game mein load kiya ja sakta hai

Zaroori limitation: OSM data mein har ghar ka exact rang, khidki, darwaza ya photo nahi hota. Yeh real map layout par bana 3D model hoga, photorealistic street scan nahi. Map data © OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL.
